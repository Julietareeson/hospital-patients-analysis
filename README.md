# Hospital Patients Analysis

Power BI project analyzing hospital patient demographics, healthcare utilization, diagnoses, and billing performance.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes hospital patient data to provide insights into patient demographics, disease patterns, hospital utilization, billing trends, and payment methods.

The analysis was designed to help hospital management understand patient patterns, identify operational trends, and support data-driven decision making.

## Business Problem

A hospital management team wants to improve patient care, optimize hospital operations, and understand financial performance. The goal of this analysis is to identify patterns in patient demographics, disease occurrence, hospital utilization, billing, and payment methods.

## Key Questions

- What are the most common diagnoses?
- Which departments receive the highest patient volume?
- Which hospitals generate the highest billing amounts?
- What is the average billing amount by diagnosis?
- How does patient volume vary over time?
- Which payment methods are used most frequently?
- What proportion of patients are admitted versus treated as outpatients?
- Are there age or gender groups with higher healthcare utilization?

The dataset was reviewed and prepared using Power Query before being loaded into Power BI.

- Data validity: 100%

 ## Analysis & DAX Measures

Several DAX measures were created to support the analysis and dashboard visualizations.

Key measures included:

- Total Patients
- Total Billing
- Average Billing
- Average Patient Age
- Admission Rate
- Admitted Patients
- Outpatients
- Male Patients
- Female Patients
- Unique Hospitals
- Unique Diagnoses
- Unique Departments

These measures were used to analyze patient volume, billing performance, admission patterns, demographics, and hospital utilization.

## Key Insights

- Upper Respiratory Tract Infection was the most common diagnosis.
- General Outpatient (OPD) recorded the highest patient volume.
- Ahmadu Bello University Teaching Hospital generated the highest total billing.
- Acute Abdomen had the highest average billing among the diagnoses analyzed.
- March recorded the highest patient volume, while May recorded the lowest.
- Cash was the most frequently used payment method.
- 40% of patients were admitted, while 60% were treated as outpatients.
- Young adults and female patients recorded higher healthcare utilization.
