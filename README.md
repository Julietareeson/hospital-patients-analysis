# Hospital Patients Analysis

Power BI project analyzing hospital patient demographics, healthcare utilization, diagnoses, and billing performance.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes hospital patient data to provide insights into patient demographics, disease patterns, hospital utilization, billing trends, and payment methods.

The analysis was designed to help hospital management understand patient patterns, identify operational trends, and support data-driven decision making.

## Business Problem

A hospital management team wants to improve patient care, optimize hospital operations, and understand financial performance. The goal of this analysis is to identify patterns in patient demographics, disease occurrence, hospital utilization, billing, and payment methods.

## Key Questions

- What are the most common diagnoses?
- Which departments receive the highest patient volume?
- Which hospitals generate the highest billing amounts?
- What is the average billing amount by diagnosis?
- How does patient volume vary over time?
- Which payment methods are used most frequently?
- What proportion of patients are admitted versus treated as outpatients?
- Are there age or gender groups with higher healthcare utilization?
